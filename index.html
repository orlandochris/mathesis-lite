Mathesis

Mathesis (μάθησις) is Greek for “learning”.

Your weekly study record, built from your Notebook sessions.

What it does

  • Takes the weekly report from each course’s Notebook and counts it for you, then lets you check it.
  • Turns it into charts and tables: what you did, how you scored, and what you struggled with.
  • Gathers the topics you struggled with into a revision list you can tick off.
  • Keeps every week on your own device, so each semester becomes a record you can look back on and download.

What it aims to do

To make studying something you can see and steer. You see where your time went, which courses need attention, and what to revise next, and you finish every semester with a complete record instead of a blur.

How to use it

  1. Open this file in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox), not inside a chat app, so your data is remembered on this device.
  2. First time? Open Customize and type in your own courses.
  3. At the end of the week, get a report from each course’s Notebook.
  4. Open Weekly, tap the course, paste the report, and press Generate.
  5. Check Revision for what to study next, and Library to look back.

How these numbers are worked out
  • Consistency: how many of the last 8 weeks have a report.
  • Coverage: how many of your courses had a report in the last 4 weeks.
  • Quiz form: your average quiz score over the last 8 weeks.
  • Practice: things done in the last 4 weeks, where 40 is the top score.
  • Revision: the share of your struggled-with topics that you have ticked as revised.
  • Initiative: personal project hours in the last 8 weeks, where 10 hours is the top score. It only counts once you log a session.

OVR is the average of the ratings that have data. A rating shows "-" until there is something to measure. The level label stays at "New profile" until you have reports in at least 2 weeks. These numbers describe your habits in this app. They are not grades.

Your place on the board

What gets shared if you join
  • Your display name and your avatar choice (a built-in avatar or the default, never your own picture).
  • Which days you opened the app in the last 30 days.
  • How many reports you saved each week (just the number).
  • Your streak and the time you were last active.

Never shared: your reports, scores, topics, courses, project sessions, or description. Leaving the board deletes your numbers from it. Other members and the app’s owner can see what is listed here.

Inviting friends (owner only)

  1. Open this app’s Share menu in Claude.
  2. Add each friend by email and give them the Contributor role. Contributors can use the app, save their own data, and join the board.
  3. Ask them to open the app, accept the agree screen, and press Join the community board here.

A plain link without an invitation usually gives outside visitors view-only access, which cannot save data or join. I could not test the exact wording of the Share menu, so check what it offers.
