# Mathesis Lite

Mathesis (μάθησις, Greek for "learning") is a personal study tracker. Once a week you tap a course, paste your weekly report, check what the app counted, and get charts, a revision list, a profile, a personal-projects page, and a semester record you can download.

This is the **Lite** version: one web page that runs in any modern browser.

- No account, no database, no AI service, no ads, no tracking.
- Everything you enter stays in your own browser on your own device.
- Download your semester as a spreadsheet file now and then. If you clear your browser data, switch browser, or use private mode, your data is gone.

## Use it

Open the page's web address in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. On first open, read and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (both are inside the app), then add your own courses under **Customize**.

## Notes

- Reports are counted by a simple reader (it looks for things like "2 quizzes" or "8/10"). Always check the numbers before saving.
- A fuller version of Mathesis runs inside Claude and can read reports with AI. That version is not part of this repository.
